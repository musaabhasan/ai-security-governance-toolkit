# Contributing

Contributions are welcome when they improve practical AI security governance.

Good contributions include:

- new control mappings
- better evidence examples
- tabletop scenarios
- education-sector AI risk examples
- policy-as-code examples
- vendor review questions
- incident response improvements

Please keep contributions vendor-neutral and practical. Avoid promotional tooling lists unless the inclusion criteria are clear.

## Contribution Checklist

- [ ] The contribution has a clear use case.
- [ ] The artifact names an owner or control objective where appropriate.
- [ ] The language is practical and audit-friendly.
- [ ] The material does not claim legal or regulatory completeness.
- [ ] External references are official or clearly marked as examples.
