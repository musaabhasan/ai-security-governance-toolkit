# AI Log Retention Policy Template

This template defines retention expectations for AI prompts, outputs, embeddings, tool calls, administrative actions, and security events.

## Policy Statements

1. AI logs must be classified before storage.
2. Sensitive data must be minimized or redacted where operationally feasible.
3. Prompt and output logs must not be used for model training unless explicitly approved.
4. Tool-call and administrative logs must be retained long enough to support incident response and audit review.
5. Access to AI logs must be restricted to approved operational, security, and assurance roles.
6. Retention exceptions must have an owner, expiration date, and compensating control.
